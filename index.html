Buyer Questions

Frequently Asked

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the product and whether it is customised. Send us your requirement and we will confirm the MOQ, pricing and lead time for your order.

Can you make private label or custom designs?

Yes. We offer private labelling, custom designs, colours, sizes and branding, including your logo on the product and packaging.

Can I order samples before a bulk order?

Yes, sample orders can be arranged so you can check quality and finish before committing to a larger order. Contact us to discuss sample terms.

Do you ship internationally?

We export worldwide. Freight, customs duties and delivery time depend on your destination country, and we will share a shipping quotation with your order.

What payment terms do you accept?

Payment terms are agreed for each order. We discuss advance and balance payment options with you before production starts.

How do you ensure quality?

Every piece is handcrafted and checked for stitching, finish, hardware and material before it is packed for dispatch.
